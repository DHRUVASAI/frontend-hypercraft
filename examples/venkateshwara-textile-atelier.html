02 · THE MANUFACTURING FLOOR

Five Disciplines From Mill Bolt to Dispatch

No outsourced job-work guesswork. Every order from 25 executive blazers to 10,000 institutional uniforms moves linearly through our in-house Hyderabad production line.
STEP 01 ISO 105-C06 AUDIT

Fabric Sourcing & GSM Lab Audit

Direct procurement from certified Indian spinning mills. Every lot is cut-tested for exact GSM mass, wet/dry rub color fastness, shrinkage tolerance (<1.5%), and warp-weft tear strength before clearing for production.

Mill-Certified Lots · Zero Sub-Grade Substitutes
STEP 02 XS TO 5XL GRADING

Custom Ergonomic Pattern Drafting

Master pattern-makers draft movement-ready blocks engineered for 10-hour airport, clinical, or factory shifts—with articulated underarm gussets, structured collars, and consistent proportional grading from XS through 5XL.

Pre-Production Sample Fit Sign-Off Included
STEP 03 MULTI-HEAD EMBROIDERY

CAD Precision Cutting & High-Density Embroidery

Zero-fray CAD nested lay cutting guarantees grainline alignment across thousands of panels. Institutional crests and corporate emblems are digitized in-house for high-density multi-head embroidery and cured plastisol screen printing.

Pantone-Matched Madeira & Rayon Thread Vault
STEP 04 12 SPI FLAT-FELLED

Industrial Double-Needle Stitching & Assembly

Dedicated Juki and Brother industrial lines execute reinforced double-needle flat-felled seams, high-tension pocket corner bar-tacking, fused collar interlinings, and shatter-resistant horn or melamine button attachment.

Bar-Tacked Stress Points · Industrial Wash Durability
STEP 05 10–10,000 UNIT LOGISTICS

Multi-Point Quality Audit & Bulk Packaging

Every finished garment passes thread-trimming, steam pressing, measurement tolerance verification, and individual size-sorted polybagging in labeled master cartons—ready for scheduled doorstep dispatch across Hyderabad, Telangana, and pan-India sites.

GST-Compliant Invoicing · Department-Wise Carton Labeling
